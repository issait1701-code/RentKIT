Book a kit ↗

  1. Choose for the task

    Start in the catalogue. Read what’s inside and what you supply. Match your phone, laptop or video source to the connections before choosing a kit.

  2. Pick up and check together

    Pick your preferred date and choose pickup or delivery. Use the contents checklist to check each item and cable before you start.

  3. Try it before the big moment

    Set up in a suitable space, check power and test the picture or recording. Keep liquids away from equipment and follow the kit’s setup checklist. Ask about a connection before assuming it works.

  4. Pack up and return

    Power down and disconnect everything. Count the contents, coil the cables and pack each piece in its place. Check the return date and report any issue with the equipment.

What is included?

Each kit has its own contents list. Video provides phone accessories; podcast provides two microphones and a recorder; movie night adds a screen and speaker; presentation includes a clicker. See the full kit details.

Do I need my own phone or laptop?

Yes: the Video Project Kit needs your compatible phone, and the Presentation Kit needs your compatible laptop. Movie Night needs your video source and content. Podcast includes a suitable recording device.

How do pickup and return work?

Select your preferred date and pickup method. Check the contents before use, then pack every item for return at the end of your selected rental period.

What should I check before renting?

Check your device ports, mount size if relevant, the required space, power access, and the contents list. We do not promise support for every phone, laptop or connector.

How is the rental price calculated?

Each package covers the full selected period: 1, 3 or 7 days. Pickup is free; delivery adds 2 000 ₸ once. Your rental summary shows the total before you review it.
