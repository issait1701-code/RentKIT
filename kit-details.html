KIT 01 / Film a project

Video Project Kit

Your phone. A steadier shot. A story worth telling.

5 000 ₸ / 1 day

Every piece has a purpose.

Phone tripod
Holds your compatible phone steady; the phone is not included.
Microphone
Captures speech for an interview or a short video.
LED light
Adds light to your scene; position it before filming.
Cables & adapters
Connect the kit to a supported phone. Check ports first.

Phone tripod · Microphone · LED light · Cables & adapters.

Your compatible phone, a charged battery and your idea.
